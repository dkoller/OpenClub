# OpenClub delivery roadmap

**Current delivery status (2026-11-19).** OpenClub is a pre-production, **one-club-per-installation** application using an independent PostgreSQL database. The synthetic member, team, role, permission, invitation, guardian, profile, team-schedule, attendance, membership-fee, Excel and announcement workflows and the German, English, French and Italian UI are implemented and exercised in CI (see the [README](../README.md#current-status-2026-11-19)). **Neither M1 hosted acceptance nor M2 real-data release has been approved.**

This is a **current delivery map**, not an implementation changelog. For the source of truth on work and acceptance checklists, use [open issues](https://github.com/dkoller/OpenClub/issues). For dated implementation evidence and the original R01–R22 sequencing, use the [archived roadmap snapshot](history/roadmap-2026-11-17.md). Original [first-milestone proposal](concepts/ai/first-milestone.md) and [MVP proposal](concepts/product/mvp.md) are historical.

## Accepted architecture and product scope

- **One club per installation**, no global person directory, club switcher or cross-club grants: [ADR-0005](decisions/0005-single-club-installation.md). Earlier [ADR-0002](decisions/0002-identity-and-membership-model.md) is superseded.
- **Both Keycloak and Rauthy remain supported** as alternative OIDC providers for an installation: [ADR-0006](decisions/0006-oidc-provider-choice.md). Disposable Rauthy test credentials and `LOCAL_TEST` are not public-hosting settings.
- A club administrator can use synthetic member search, create, full edit (version-checked), details, archive/reactivate and export, teams and assignments, role changes, scoped grant/revoke and basic club settings. Public display names follow the accepted rule: adults' full names; minors' first name and surname initial. Private fields and production privacy controls need independent approval.
- **Issue audit 2026-11-19:** #13 (versioned contract), #17 (settings), #18 (member endpoints), #20 (invitations and linking) and #25 (browser journeys) were closed with evidence against `main`; their only remaining item, the hosted demo, is tracked in [#28](https://github.com/dkoller/OpenClub/issues/28). #27 stays open for the real host's cost and resource figures; #26 (export, deletion, audit) was completed by the editor export (OpenAPI 1.7.0).
- First-release teams and configurable permission grants are implemented for synthetic data ([#75](https://github.com/dkoller/OpenClub/issues/75), [#76](https://github.com/dkoller/OpenClub/issues/76), closed 2026-11-17; remaining contract and test gates are in #13, #24 and #25); invitations/account linking ([#20](https://github.com/dkoller/OpenClub/issues/20)) are part of the hosted M1 demo by owner decision of 2026-11-18 (design and contract approved; services, HTTP routes, DE/EN/FR/IT UI and a Keycloak browser journey are built, a Keycloak and a Rauthy browser journey are built, hosted evidence is pending; a guardian signing in for a minor needs its own design and privacy review first). Accounting, chat, tournaments and integration features remain post-MVP ([#31](https://github.com/dkoller/OpenClub/issues/31)). By owner decision of 2026-11-19 ([ADR-0008 addendum 12](decisions/0008-owner-decisions-2026-11-18.md)) four features are built before pilot feedback, each after its own contract approval: My profile (OpenAPI 1.9.0), trainings and events (1.10.0), attendance (1.11.0) and membership fees (1.12.0) are built; by [addendum 16](decisions/0008-owner-decisions-2026-11-18.md) the Excel member list (1.13.0) and announcements (1.14.0) are built.

**Never infer production readiness or authorization to enter real member information from green CI or a merged procedure.** Production credentials, spending, operator actions and privacy/legal approvals require separate explicit authorization.
